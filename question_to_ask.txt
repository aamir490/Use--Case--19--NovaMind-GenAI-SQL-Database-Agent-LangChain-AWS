NovaMind AI — Questions to Ask
================================
Use these in the Streamlit chat UI or POST to /query.
Select the matching data source in the sidebar before asking.

─────────────────────────────────────
 LIBRARY DATA  (Data Source: Athena)
─────────────────────────────────────

1. How many books are in the library?

2. How many distinct genres are there in the library?

3. List all books written by H. G. Wells.

4. Which genre has the most books?

5. How many books were published after 1950?

6. Who are the top 5 authors with the most books in the library?

7. List all Science Fiction books ordered by publication date.

8. How many books were published in each decade?

─────────────────────────────────────
 CARS DATA  (Data Source: Athena)
─────────────────────────────────────

9.  How many cars are in the dataset?

10. What is the average price of a car?

11. Which car make has the highest average horsepower?

12. List all cars with more than 200 horsepower.

13. What are the top 5 most fuel-efficient cars by mpg?

14. How many cars are available for each body style?

15. What is the average price of rear-wheel drive cars compared to front-wheel drive cars?
